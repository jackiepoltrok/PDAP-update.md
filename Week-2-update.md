This week we started thinking about our PDAPs together in groups. My group, which was interested in human debris and direct human impact on different parts of campus, discussed things we can physically track like stickers, posters, QR codes, desire paths, graffiti, tags, etc. 
For my PDAP, I am thinking of looking at gum stuck to tables and chairs in a classroom on campus. I want to choose only one classroom because looking at multiple might not be practical. But I think looking at gum placement can tell you a lot about the way students interact with classrooms. Where do student sit more? At the back of the class or the front? Are there different types of tables or chairs in the room that might be favoured by students? How often is the gum in classrooms being cleaned and removed? What kind of classes usually take place in this class?
There are a lot of questions to consider with this idea but I think it could be an interesting approach.
