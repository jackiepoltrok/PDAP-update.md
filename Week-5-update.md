I needed to rethink my PDAP and I needed a little help doing so. After completing the first consolidation document in class, I asked Kavita for a little help with my PDAP.
Initially, my idea was to create a GIS map of a classroom in Tory Building, with it being the oldest building on campus, and document all of the gum stuck under the chairs and desks to discover patterns of classroom behaviour.
Then I did the GIS mapping workshop and realized that may not be the route for me. I still think it is a great idea, but I don't have much confidence that I would be able to pull that off in the next few weeks.
What I also know, is that I had a really fun time doing photogrammetry with Kavita at the Billings Estate. She had mentioned to me and a few other students that there are apps we can use to create digital 3D models of objects on our own time. In class I asked Kavita about these apps and changed up the idea of my PDAP.
Now, I am going to try to create 3D models of my mom's old digital camera, that I now use as my own which is an important historical object for me, using three different photogrammetry apps on my phone. I am going to compare and contrast the results and ethics of the three apps in relation to the fundamentals of digital archaeology.
